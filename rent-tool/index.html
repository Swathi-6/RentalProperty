Limits of this tool

  • "Last rented" is an assumption. Zillow shows the price when a listing was removed. That usually means a lease was signed, but it could also mean the owner pulled the listing. No public source shows signed lease amounts.
  • The comps are mostly priced by large companies (FirstKey Homes, Main Street Renewal, Progress Residential) that start high and cut on a schedule. Their asking rents describe their pricing method as much as tenant demand.
  • Adjustment amounts are judgment, not measured. Five comps are too few to estimate what a bathroom or a square foot is worth here, so the defaults are round numbers you can change.
  • Interior condition is unknown. Listing photos and a 2012 remodel note are all there is. A visit would settle the basement and condition questions.
  • The vacancy table does not predict how long a price takes to rent. It tells you how much extra vacancy a higher price can absorb before it loses money. Whether the market gives you that time is the risk this home's own history shows.
  • Not covered: concessions, pet rent ($30/mo here), application fees, lease-length incentives, and the cost of turnover itself.
